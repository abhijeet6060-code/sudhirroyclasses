Company Secretary Government Jobs: SEBI, PSU Recruitment, Salary & Exam Preparation

✍️ By Prof. Sudhir Rai (20+ Years Teaching Experience) 📅 Updated September 2026 ⏱️ 8 Min Read
💡 Key Career Takeaway: A Qualified Company Secretary (CS) is not restricted to corporate boardrooms. Top Central Government Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs like ONGC, IOCL, BHEL, GAIL, Coal India), regulatory authorities (SEBI, RBI, IBBI, CCI), and the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) regularly recruit Company Secretaries with starting pay scales ranging between ₹12 LPA to ₹25+ LPA along with gazetted government perks and lifetime job security.

What is the Meaning & Statutory Role of a Company Secretary (CS)?

Under Section 203 of the Companies Act, 2013, a Company Secretary is recognized as Key Managerial Personnel (KMP) alongside the Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer. A CS acts as the chief governance officer, legal advisor to the board of directors, compliance strategist, and statutory bridge between the company, stakeholders, and regulatory bodies like the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI), and National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT).

Because all listed companies and large public corporations in India are legally mandated to maintain flawless compliance, the demand for qualified Company Secretaries in both public and private sectors has experienced a massive surge.

Top Government Jobs for Company Secretaries in India

Government departments, statutory bodies, and financial regulatory authorities hire Company Secretaries across executive and managerial cadres through direct notifications and competitive recruitment exams:

1. SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India) - Grade A Assistant Manager

SEBI conducts recruitment for Officer Grade A (Assistant Manager) in Legal, General, and Research streams. Company Secretaries with strong command over securities laws, SEBI (LODR) regulations, insider trading norms, and corporate takeovers have an exceptional advantage in clearing both Phase 1 and Phase 2 examinations.

2. Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) & Official Liquidators

The MCA recruits Company Secretaries for technical legal positions including Assistant Registrar of Companies (AROC), Company Prosecutors, and Technical Assistants in the Serious Fraud Investigation Office (SFIO) and National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) benches.

3. Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & Financial Institutions

RBI Grade B (Legal/General), NABARD, SIDBI, and EXIM Bank recruit CS professionals in their corporate governance, compliance, and secretarial departments to oversee FEMA guidelines and banking regulations.

4. Competition Commission of India (CCI) & IBBI

The Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI) and Competition Commission of India hire CS professionals for policy analysis, corporate restructuring audits, and competition advocacy.

PSU Recruitment for Company Secretaries (Maharatna & Navratna)

Central Public Sector Enterprises (CPSEs) hire qualified CS members through competitive selections, written tests, and technical interviews. Major recruiting PSUs include:

  • Energy & Petroleum PSUs: Oil and Natural Gas Corporation (ONGC), Indian Oil Corporation Limited (IOCL), Bharat Petroleum (BPCL), GAIL India, NTPC.
  • Mining & Heavy Industries: Coal India Limited (CIL), Steel Authority of India (SAIL), Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited (BHEL), NMDC.
  • Infrastructure & Finance: Power Grid Corporation of India (PGCIL), REC Limited, PFC Limited, National Highways Authority of India (NHAI).

Company Secretary Exam Structure & Core Subjects (ICSI Syllabus)

To become a qualified CS eligible for these coveted PSU and government positions, candidates must clear the 3-tier examination conducted by the Institute of Company Secretaries of India (ICSI):

Stage 1: CSEET (CS Executive Entrance Test)

Entry-level test covering Business Communication, Legal Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, Economic & Business Environment, and Current Affairs.

Stage 2: CS Executive Examination (Core Law Mastery)

  • Group 1: Jurisprudence, Interpretation & General Laws (JIGL), Company Law & Practice, Setting Up of Business, Industrial & Labour Laws.
  • Group 2: Corporate Accounting and Financial Management, Capital Markets & Securities Laws (CMSL), Economic, Commercial & Intellectual Property Laws (ECIPL), Tax Laws & Practice (Direct Tax & GST).

Stage 3: CS Professional Examination (Advanced Strategic Governance)

Advanced modules covering Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) - Principles & Practice, Drafting, Pleadings & Appearances, Compliance Management, Audit & Due Diligence, and Corporate Restructuring & Insolvency.

🎯 Preparation Insight by Prof. Sudhir Rai:
PSU selection boards and SEBI interview panels test your real-time interpretation of Secretarial Standards (SS-1 and SS-2), SEBI LODR Regulations, IBC 2016, and Corporate Direct Taxes. Memorizing bare acts is not enough; conceptual clarity and clause-by-clause drafting skills are mandatory.

How to Prepare for CS Executive & Professional to Crack Govt Roles?

  1. Focus on Bare Act Clause Analysis: Avoid relying solely on summary notes. Read the bare text of the Companies Act 2013 and SEBI Regulations alongside corresponding MCA notification rules.
  2. Master Taxation & Corporate Accounting: PSU examinations include rigorous practical sections in Corporate Direct Tax (MAT Sec 115JB, Capital Gains) and GST Compliance.
  3. Solve RTPs, MTPs & Previous 10-Term Papers: Weekly written mock tests are essential to master answer presentation under strict ICSI exam timing constraints.
  4. Enroll in Dedicated Offline Classroom Batches: Learn directly under expert faculty with structured classroom doubt solving and daily questioning sessions.
