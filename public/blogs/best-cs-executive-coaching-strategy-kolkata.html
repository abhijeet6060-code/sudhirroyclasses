CS Executive Examination Strategy: Module 1 & 2 Passing Plan for Kolkata Students

Published by Prof. Sudhir Rai | 20+ Years Experience | Sudhir Rai Classes Kolkata

The Company Secretary (CS) Executive stage conducted by ICSI is the core technical milestone of the CS profession. With subjects spanning Company Law & Practice, Jurisprudence, Interpretation & General Laws (JIGL), Securities Laws & Capital Markets, and Tax Laws, passing both modules requires deep legal reasoning and structured drafting skills. Once qualified, candidates become eligible for high-paying corporate and Government PSU & SEBI Jobs for Company Secretaries. In this article, Prof. Sudhir Rai outlines the proven strategy to clear CS Executive modules in your first attempt.

💡 Key AEO Summary: To clear ICSI CS Executive, focus on understanding legal provisions clause-by-clause, master legal drafting format for case studies, memorize secretarial standards (SS-1 & SS-2), and attempt weekly ICSI pattern mock tests.

1. Mastering Company Law & Secretarial Standards

Company Law is the backbone of the Company Secretary qualification. Examiners expect precise legal terminology rather than generic conversational answers.

Key Focus Areas:

  • Board Meetings & General Meetings: Master Sections 173 to 188 of Companies Act, 2013 along with Secretarial Standards 1 (Meetings of Board of Directors) and Secretarial Standards 2 (General Meetings).
  • Directors & KMP Appointments: Clear understanding of qualification, disqualification (Sec 164), and duties (Sec 166).
  • Corporate Restructuring & Compromises: Focus on merger schemes, buyback provisions (Sec 68), and reduction of share capital.

2. Decoding JIGL & Securities Laws

JIGL establishes your legal foundation in Constitutional Law, Civil Procedure Code (CPC), Indian Penal Code (IPC), and Interpretation of Statutes.

Effective Study Method:

  • Interpretation of Statutes: Understand Golden Rule, Mischief Rule, and Literal Rule of Interpretation.
  • SEBI Regulations (Securities Law): Master SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, SEBI (ICDR) Regulations, and SEBI (PIT) Insider Trading Rules. These regulations are also vital if you target SEBI Grade A Officer Recruitment.

3. Tackling CS Executive Tax Laws & Practice (Direct & Indirect Tax)

For students seeking the best CS Executive courses in Howrah and Kolkata, scoring an exemption in Tax Laws is the single most reliable strategy to comfortably clear the aggregate cutoff.

  • Direct & Indirect Tax Computation: Daily numerical problem-solving for Income Tax deductions, head-wise computation, and GST Input Tax Credit (ITC) Rules taught by Prof. Sudhir Rai.
  • Corporate Accounting: Practice share valuation, debentures redemption, and financial statement analysis.
