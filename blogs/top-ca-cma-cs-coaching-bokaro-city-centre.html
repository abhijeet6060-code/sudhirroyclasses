3. Why Specialisation in Law & Tax is the Ultimate Winning Edge

In all three courses — CA, CMA, and CS — the biggest stumbling block for 80% of students is the non-numerical or semi-numerical legal papers:

  • Direct Tax (Income Tax Act 1961): 298 Sections, dozens of Schedules, retrospective budget amendments, and confusing deductions.
  • Indirect Tax (CGST, SGST, IGST, UTGST, Customs): Valuation rules, complex input tax credit restrictions under Section 17(5), and place of supply logic.
  • Corporate & Economic Laws: Intricate wording of the Companies Act 2013, meeting quorums, Director disqualifications, and penalties.
