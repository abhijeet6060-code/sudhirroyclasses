1. Demystifying Direct Tax (Income Tax) & GST

Taxation is divided into Direct Tax (Income Tax Act, 1961) and Indirect Tax (Goods & Services Tax - GST). Most students fail to score high marks because they treat Taxation as a theoretical subject rather than a practical numerical discipline.

Core Strategies for Direct Tax:

  • Heads of Income Mastery: Focus intensely on Profits & Gains of Business or Profession (PGBP) and Capital Gains. Solves at least 25 comprehensive computation problems for total income.
  • Deductions & Tax Rates: Memorize Chapter VI-A deductions (Section 80C to 80U) along with default tax regime vs optional tax regime computations.
  • TDS & TCS Provisions: Master threshold limits and deduction rates under Section 194.

Core Strategies for GST (Scoring Section):

  • Input Tax Credit (ITC): Understand Section 16, 17(5) blocked credits, and ITC utilization ordering. GST is 40 marks and highly scoring if concepts are clear.
  • Place & Time of Supply: Practice numerical questions on determining value of supply under Section 15.

2. Conquering Corporate & Other Laws

Corporate Law requires precise legal phrasing, section citations, and case law applications. Rote learning without understanding provisions leads to average scores.

Step-by-Step Law Preparation:

  • Companies Act, 2013: Focus on Management & Administration, Declaration & Payment of Dividend, Accounts of Companies, and Audit & Auditors.
  • Section Number Tricks: Maintain a separate formula & section notebook. At Sudhir Rai Classes, daily random class questioning reinforces section retention.
  • Case Law Drafting: Structure your answers in 3 clear parts: (1) Relevant Provision & Section, (2) Analysis of Facts, and (3) Clear Conclusion.
